Frequently AskedQuestions

Which videos work best with Kairos?

Podcasts, lectures, interviews, vlogs and gaming videos. Kairos detects the genre and scores clips with weights made for that genre.

How does Kairos decide which clip is the best?

A model scores each candidate on hook, self-containment, emotional peak, pacing, retention risk and genre fit. Code combines the scores into one total, so the ranking is repeatable.

Will generated product photos change my product?

No. Every image passes label OCR, shape, colour and similarity checks. If a check fails, Kairos pastes your real product back in or regenerates the shot.

Can I turn a video into a LinkedIn carousel?

Yes. Pick a finished video job as the source, and Kairos plans the slides from its transcript.
